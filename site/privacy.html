Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026.

Mantel is software you install and run on your own computer. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it, including when you sign in with Google.

Where your information lives

Everything Mantel stores (settings, calendar events it has fetched, lists, photos, and the sign-in tokens Google gives it) is kept in Mantel's data folder on the computer that runs it. Sign-in tokens and passwords are encrypted there. The people who make Mantel never receive, see or store any of it. There is no Mantel server, account or analytics.

Google user data

When you connect a Google account, Mantel asks Google for:

  • Your calendar list (read only), so you can choose which calendars to show.
  • Events on your calendars, so the display can show them, and add, edit or delete an event when you do that on the display or the phone page.
  • Photos you pick in the Google Photos picker (read only), so they can appear in the slideshow. Mantel cannot see any photo you did not pick.

This data goes only between Google and the Mantel on your computer, and is shown only on your own screens. It is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Mantel's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services you connect

Weather, travel times, Home Assistant, iCloud and other integrations are optional. When you turn one on, your Mantel talks to that service directly with the details you entered. Their own privacy policies apply to what they receive.

Removing your data

Disconnect Google in Mantel's Settings, or remove Mantel's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting Mantel's data folder removes everything it stored.
