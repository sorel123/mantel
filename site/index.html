Mantel

A family wall display you run yourself: calendars, weather, photos, lists, chores and Home Assistant controls on a tablet or spare screen.

Mantel runs on a computer in your home (Docker, a Windows PC, a Mac or Linux box). Your calendars, photos and settings stay on that computer. There is no Mantel account and no Mantel cloud service.

Signing in with Google

When you connect Google Calendar or pick photos from Google Photos, Google asks whether Mantel may see your calendars and the photos you choose. Mantel uses that only to show and edit them on your own display. Read the privacy policy.
