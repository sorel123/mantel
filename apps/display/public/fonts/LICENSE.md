# Bundled fonts

Latin subsets of variable fonts, served from Google Fonts' releases. All are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).

- Fraunces (Undercase Type)
- Manrope (Mikhail Sharanda)
- Space Grotesk (Florian Karsten)
- Lora (Cyreal)
- Caveat (Impallari Type)
- Archivo (Omnibus-Type)
